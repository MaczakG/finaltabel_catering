Event Organisation

From idea to execution.

Whether it's an intimate ten-person seated diplomatic dinner, a corporate gala for several hundred guests, or an exclusive private event, our task is the same: precise planning and flawless execution of the entire kitchen and logistics operation.

We don't work from a catalogue. We build the menu, service, and infrastructure around the venue's character, your guests' needs, and the rhythm of the event. From striking presentation to precise service, we coordinate everything so the event reads as one seamless, professional whole from the first minute to the last.

  • Seated Dinners & Galas: The best of classic Hungarian cuisine alongside international favourites, executed to perfection. We bring generous, rich portions and full, home-style flavours to the table, with presentation and service protocol that holds its own at an elegant gala.
  • Standing Receptions & Finger Food: Elegant, easy-to-eat yet substantial bites and small-format culinary compositions. Thanks to our continuous kitchen support, the spread stays fresh, abundant, and beautifully presented throughout the entire event.
  • Business & Diplomatic Events: Discreet, protocol-perfect presence at high-level negotiations and delegation receptions. Service is precise to the minute and unobtrusive, keeping the focus on the discussions at all times.
  • Family & Private Events: Birthdays, anniversaries, engagements, as well as stag and hen parties, where a relaxed atmosphere meets premium hospitality. Portions are never scaled back: we tailor the menu entirely to the group's taste — whether that means generous, full-flavoured Hungarian dishes or striking food served live on site.

Everything from a ten-person seated diplomatic dinner to a corporate gala for several hundred guests — we always scale the team and logistics to match the guest count.
